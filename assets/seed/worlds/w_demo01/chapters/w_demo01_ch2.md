# Chapter 2 — The Ride to Duskmere

Kaelen left Velenhold at dawn and reached Duskmere before the sun went down.

He pushed the grey mare hard across the Ashen Flats, and the miles fell away under her hooves. By the time the crooked roofs of Duskmere rose out of the ash, the sky was only beginning to redden in the west. A single day's ride, and he was home.

The town square was crowded when the raiders came.

There was no time to wait for dark. Kaelen reached for the nearest flame — a brazier burning outside the smithy — and bound it, there in the full light of the afternoon sun. Fire leapt from his fingers in a bright arc, and the lead raider fell screaming. The noon light did nothing to dim it; if anything the flames burned hotter for the daylight, and the crowd scattered from the heat.

On the back of his right hand, the Embermark blazed white. He had never bound a flame so large. He felt a memory tear loose and vanish — he could not even tell which one — and then it was done, and the square was full of smoke and the smell of char.

A girl's voice cut through it. He turned, his heart already knowing.
