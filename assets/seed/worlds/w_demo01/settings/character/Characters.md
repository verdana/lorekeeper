# Characters

## Kaelen Vshare — protagonist

**Age:** 19.
**Eyes:** grey — a pale, ashen grey, often remarked upon as unsettling.
**Embermark:** on the left hand.

Kaelen is the youngest Emberwright ever admitted to the courts of Velenhold. Orphaned young, raised by the archivists of the Deep Library, he is quiet, careful, and terrified of what his own power costs him — every binding steals a memory, and he has already forgotten his mother's face.

He carries a single obsession: to find out who betrayed the Covenant and set the northern provinces to burn.

## Mirae Vshare — Kaelen's sister

**Age:** 14.
Kaelen's younger sister, **Mirae**, is not an Emberwright. She is sharp-tongued, fearless, and the only person who can pull Kaelen back from the hollowness that deep bindings leave behind. She keeps a written record of his memories so that when he forgets, she can tell him who he is.

Her name is always **Mirae** — never shortened.

## Seraphine Dain — the Ashen General

**Age:** 41.
Seraphine Dain was the greatest Emberwright general of her generation — until she **died at the Siege of Velenhold**, three winters ago, burned in the collapse of the North Gate. Her death is the wound at the center of the story: it was her killing, in violation of the Covenant, that began the unraveling.

She is dead. She appears in the story only through memory, record, and the grief of those who knew her.
