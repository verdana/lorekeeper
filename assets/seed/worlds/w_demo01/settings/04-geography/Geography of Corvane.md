# Geography of Corvane

## Velenhold — the capital

The seat of the Emberwright courts, a walled city built around the Deep Library. Its North Gate collapsed during the Siege three winters ago and has never been rebuilt.

## Duskmere — the southern town

**Duskmere lies three days' ride south of Velenhold**, across the Ashen Flats. There is no faster route: the Flats cannot be crossed at speed, and no rider has ever made Duskmere from the capital in less than three days.

Duskmere is where Kaelen was born, and where the story begins.

## The Ashen Flats

A grey plain of cold ash between Velenhold and Duskmere, left behind by an ancient fire that burned for a century. Nothing grows there. Crossing it takes the better part of three days on horseback.
