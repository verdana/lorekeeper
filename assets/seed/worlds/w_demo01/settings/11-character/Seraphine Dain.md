## Background

The greatest Emberwright general of her generation — until she **died at the Siege of Velenhold**, three winters ago, burned in the collapse of the North Gate. Her death is the wound at the center of the story: it was her killing, in violation of the Covenant, that began the unraveling.

She is dead. She appears in the story only through memory, record, and the grief of those who knew her.
