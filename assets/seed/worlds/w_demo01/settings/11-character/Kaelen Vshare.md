# Kaelen Vshare

## Basic Information

- **Full Name**: Kaelen Vshare
- **Alias / Titles**: —
- **Age**: 19
- **Gender**: Male
- **Affiliation**: Emberwrights, the courts of Velenhold
- **Role**: Protagonist

## Appearance

Eyes are a pale, ashen grey, often remarked upon as unsettling. Carries an Embermark on the left hand.

## Personality

Quiet, careful, and terrified of what his own power costs him — every binding steals a memory, and he has already forgotten his mother's face.

## Background

The youngest Emberwright ever admitted to the courts of Velenhold. Orphaned young, raised by the archivists of the Deep Library. He carries a single obsession: to find out who betrayed the Covenant and set the northern provinces to burn.

## Abilities & Skills

- Emberwright: reads the traces of fire; every binding costs a memory.

## Relationships

- **Mirae Vshare** — his younger sister, the only person who can pull him back from the hollowness that deep bindings leave behind.

## Arc & Development

Hunts the traitor who broke the three-hundred-year Covenant and set the north to burn.
