# The Emberwright's Covenant

The world of **Corvane** runs on a single, unbreakable law: fire remembers. Every flame that has ever burned holds a trace of what it consumed, and those who learn to read those traces are called **Emberwrights**.

## The Three Laws of Ember

1. **Emberwrights can only cast at night.** Sunlight nullifies their power completely — under direct daylight, an Emberwright is no more than an ordinary person. This is why the great battles of the age are fought after dusk.
2. **Every binding costs a memory.** To bind a flame to one's will erases a single memory from the caster, chosen at random. Powerful Emberwrights are often hollow, forgetful people who have traded away their own pasts.
3. **Fire cannot be created, only borrowed.** An Emberwright must draw from an existing flame; they cannot conjure fire from nothing.

## The Embermark

Those born with the gift bear the **Embermark** — a burn-like scar that appears on the **left hand** at the moment their power first wakes. The mark glows faintly when a flame is near. It cannot be removed or hidden; it is the surest sign of an Emberwright.

## The Covenant

Long ago the Emberwrights swore the Covenant: never to use their gift to kill, in exchange for their place in the courts of Velenhold. The Covenant has held for three hundred years. It is beginning to break.
